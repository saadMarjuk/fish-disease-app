Account & Data Control

Delete Your Account.

Fish Disease Detection users can permanently delete their account and associated stored data directly from the application or request deletion by email if they cannot access the app.

Important: Account deletion is permanent. Once the account and associated data have been deleted, they may not be recoverable.

01

Delete Your Account Inside the App

The easiest way to delete your Fish Disease Detection account is directly from the mobile application.

Open the application while signed in and navigate to:

Application Menu

More → Delete Account

Follow the confirmation instructions shown by the application.

02

Data Removed During Account Deletion

When account deletion is successfully completed, the application attempts to permanently remove data associated with the user's account.

This may include:

  • Firebase Authentication account
  • User profile information stored in Firestore
  • Stored fish-disease detection history
  • Account-related application data associated with the user

03

Cannot Access the App?

If you cannot access the application or cannot use the in-app Delete Account feature, you can request account deletion by email.

For account security, send the request from the email address associated with your Fish Disease Detection account whenever possible.

Developer: MARJUK IBNA BELAYET

Email: saadmarjukibnabelayet@gmail.com

Recommended subject: Fish Disease Detection - Account Deletion Request

Send Deletion Request

04

What to Include in Your Request

To help identify the correct account, include the email address associated with your Fish Disease Detection account.

Do not send passwords, authentication codes, private keys, payment information, or other sensitive credentials.

05

Identity Verification

A deletion request may require reasonable verification before the account is removed.

This helps prevent another person from requesting deletion of an account that does not belong to them.

06

After Deletion

After a valid account deletion request has been successfully processed, the associated account and stored application data covered by the request will be removed.

Certain technical information may be retained only where required for security, legal, fraud-prevention, or technical purposes, if applicable.

07

Fish Images

Fish images submitted for prediction may be transmitted to the application's external machine-learning service for processing.

For additional information about image processing, Firebase, Firestore, and application data practices, review the Privacy Policy.

08

Educational Project

Fish Disease Detection is an academic and educational application created for learning, research, demonstration, and portfolio purposes.

It is not operated as a commercial veterinary service.
