Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy.

This page explains how Fish Disease Detection collects, uses, stores, processes, and protects information while providing its educational fish-disease prediction features.

Last Updated: September 24, 2026

Fish Disease Detection is an academic and educational project created for learning, research, demonstration, and portfolio purposes.

This application is not operated as a commercial veterinary service or business platform.

01

Information We Collect

When you create an account, the application may collect and store:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your Firebase user ID
  • Account creation information

This information is used to create, authenticate, identify, and manage your application account.

02

Fish Images and Disease Prediction

When you select a fish image and request a disease prediction, the selected image is transmitted to an external machine-learning prediction service for analysis.

The image is used for processing the disease prediction requested by the user.

Users should not upload images containing personal, private, sensitive, or unrelated information.

03

Detection History

For signed-in users, Fish Disease Detection may store prediction history using Google Firestore.

Stored detection information may include:

  • Fish type
  • Predicted disease
  • Prediction confidence
  • Disease description
  • Treatment or advice information
  • Prevention information
  • Date and time of prediction

This information is stored so users can view previous prediction results from their account.

04

Firebase Services

The application uses Google Firebase services for:

  • User authentication
  • Account management
  • Firestore database storage
  • Detection history storage

Authentication and related application data are handled through Firebase services where applicable.

05

How We Use Your Information

Information collected by the application is used only for purposes related to application functionality, including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts
  • Authenticating users
  • Providing fish-disease predictions
  • Displaying prediction results
  • Saving prediction history
  • Displaying previous detection results
  • Maintaining normal application functionality

Fish Disease Detection does not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

06

Third-Party Services

The application uses third-party services necessary for its operation, including:

  • Google Firebase
  • An external machine-learning prediction service used for fish-disease analysis

Data required to provide these features may be transmitted to the relevant service when users use that functionality.

07

Data Retention

Account information and stored prediction history may remain available while the user's account remains active.

Users may request or perform account deletion as described below.

08

Account and Data Deletion

Users can delete their account directly from the application by going to:

More → Delete Account

When account deletion is successfully completed, the application attempts to remove:

  • The user's Firebase Authentication account
  • The user's Firestore profile data
  • The user's stored detection history

If a user cannot access the application, they may request deletion through the public Account Deletion page.

Open Account Deletion Page →

09

Educational and Non-Commercial Purpose

Fish Disease Detection was developed as an academic and educational project.

It is not intended to operate as a commercial veterinary service, paid consultation platform, or professional diagnostic service.

Demonstration veterinary profiles shown inside the application are sample content used for educational purposes and should not be treated as verified professional veterinary services.

10

Veterinary and Educational Disclaimer

Disease predictions, descriptions, treatment suggestions, prevention information, and other content provided by the application are intended for educational and informational purposes only.

They should not be treated as a substitute for professional veterinary diagnosis, treatment, or advice.

Users dealing with serious fish-health problems should consult a qualified veterinary or aquaculture professional.

11

Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect application and account information through services such as Firebase Authentication and Firestore.

However, no internet-connected system can guarantee complete security.

12

Children's Privacy

Fish Disease Detection is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

Users should follow applicable age requirements and local laws when using the application.

13

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features, third-party services, or data practices change.

The latest version will remain publicly available on this page.
